# R&D / Prototyping Engineer

This repository tracks my development as an **R&D / Prototyping Engineer**, with a focus on turning technical uncertainty into working prototypes, tools, and validated solutions.

My working style combines **Unreal Engine, C++, Blueprints, custom tooling, plugins, and AI as a constant engineering companion** for research, exploration, implementation, debugging, documentation, and communication.

## Focus

- Unreal Engine
- C++
- Rapid prototyping
- Technical research
- Tools development
- Systems experimentation
- AI-assisted engineering workflows
- New technologies and workflows

## What I want to get better at

- Exploring unfamiliar technical problems quickly
- Understanding requirements, constraints, context, and expected timelines
- Building prototypes to validate ideas
- Comparing possible technical approaches
- Identifying risks early
- Turning research into practical implementations
- Building reusable tools and plugins
- Documenting technical decisions and lessons learned
- Explaining how something works, why it was built that way, and what it is useful for
- Communicating results through technical notes, presentations, and demo videos

## Evidence

This repository will gradually collect:

- Experiments
- Prototypes
- Research notes
- Technical investigations
- Case studies
- Lessons learned from real problems
- Presentations and demo material

The goal is not to collect theory, but to build a visible record of **how I investigate, prototype, validate, solve, and communicate technical problems**.
